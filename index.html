Why Computer Engineering?

Career Opportunities

01

SOFTWARE ENGINEER

Designs, builds, and maintains software applications and systems.

02

HARDWARE SYSTEM ENGINEER

Develops and manages physical computer components and integrated hardware systems.

03

NETWORK ENGINEER

Sets up, configures, and maintains computer networks to ensure reliable communication.

04

WEB DEVELOPER

Creates and manages websites and web applications.

05

SOFTWARE SECURITY ENGINEER

Protects software systems from cyber threats by finding and fixing vulnerabilities.

06

GAME DEVELOPER

Designs and develops video games, including gameplay, graphics, and mechanics.

Frequently Asked

What is Computer Engineering?

+
Computer engineering is the branch of engineering that integrates electronics engineering with computer sciences. Computer Engineers design and develop computer systems and other technological devices.

Why choose Computer Engineering?

+
You can have multiple job opportunities. It opens opportunities for high salary jobs. It challenges you because of the rapid growth of technology. You can express your creativity and innovation. You have limitless future opportunities.
